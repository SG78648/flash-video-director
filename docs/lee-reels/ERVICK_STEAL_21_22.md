## What I stole, as structure and not as words

Stealing like an artist means taking the move and putting your own material in it. Nothing below uses his wording, his numbers or his deals.
1. **A very specific viewer.** His headline names one person (high income tech earners) and one outcome. A sentence that names its viewer is shared by that viewer. For Lee the viewer is already in the plan (9 to 5ers, and people who buy on the spreadsheet).
2. **Quote the industry's own words, then show what it actually does.** His fee post lines up the nice phrase ("renter experience") against the practice (fees in fine print). That gap is cognitive dissonance in two sentences. Both scripts do it. "Generous" rent against a building that rots. "Underwriting" against a number he knew was too low.
3. **Name the thing the industry avoids.** Fees for him. Below market rent and best case underwriting for Lee.
4. **Proof from a real deal, with a figure, in the first breath.** He leads with equity deployed and portfolio size. Lee leads with a real building and a real number.
5. **Show the bad news first.** His transparency principle. Both reels include the cost (tenants complained and some left, the extra gap he refused to count on).

## Where each fact comes from (Lee to confirm)

All from his own LinkedIn posts.
- 21 is the post about raising rents on the 12 unit building seven years ago. $2,400 is my arithmetic ($200 x 12 units) and assumes every unit was $200 below market. If only some were, change the number. He should say whether he is happy with "isn't kind, it's how a building rots", which is stronger than his own line ("a landlord who's afraid to charge market rent isn't being generous").
- 22 is the post on 15 years running kitchens and the 128 unit Mississippi building. $300 is the gap at the low end of $850 to $950, and $38,400 is $300 x 128. Whether the 128 units are the same building as the seller note deal in reel 14 is not stated, so do not say they are.
- Rent increases are a sensitive subject. Check local rent rules and notice periods before posting 21, and keep the tone on repairs and fairness.

## If everything depended on it

Nothing can make a reel viral, and a plan that claims it can is selling something. What these two do is give every second a reason, a dissonant first line, a real receipt, a delayed answer and one clear send. That is the shape that did best on the other accounts. The next step with the most value is the one above, his top post links, so the next round is built on what actually worked for him and not on what a profile and two podcasts suggest.
