## 1. What the numbers say

Under 100 views with 276 followers means Instagram tried each reel on a small slice of his followers and almost nobody stayed past the opening seconds.
So the problem is not the niche, the hashtags or the posting time. It is the first two seconds and the lack of anything worth watching to the end.
Two reels a day with the same message made it worse, because every new reel is judged against the failed one before it.

What this means for goals. The first goal is not viral. It is to get a normal reel from under 100 views to a few hundred, then to find the one format that gets sent.
A reel that is sent by even five strangers is worth more than a hundred likes from friends.

The hook language of a 9 to 5er is time, a boss, a commute, a salary and Sunday evening. Use those words in the first line.

## 5. The DEAL auto reply

### How it must work
- The comment must contain the word DEAL. The tool sends one private reply to that comment.
- Meta allows a private reply within 7 days of the comment and only one per comment. Further messages need the person to answer first, then a 24 hour window applies. Details are in the [Meta documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/private-replies).
- So the first message must end with a question. Without a question there is no second message.
- The account has to be a public professional account or the automation silently fails.
- Use an official tool such as ManyChat. Do not use anything that logs in with his password.

### Copy, ready to paste
Public reply to the comment, rotate these three so it does not look robotic.
1. "Sent it to your DMs. Check the requests folder if you don't see it."
2. "Done, it's in your inbox. Tell me what you think."
3. "On its way to your DMs now."

First message.
"Hey, thanks for commenting DEAL. Here is the 9 to 5 owner pack with the first step checklist and the lot math sheet. [link]
Quick question so I send the right things next. Are you A) learning the basics, B) looking at your first deal, or C) you already own something? Just reply with A, B or C."

If A. "Great place to start. The first step is learning to read one deal. Here is a short video that walks through one with real numbers. [link to a reel]. Send me the first number that confuses you and I'll explain it."
If B. "Nice. Send me the price, the income and the expenses of the deal and I'll tell you what questions I'd ask before going further. Educational only, not advice."
If C. "Good. What is it, and what would you change if you could start again? I'm collecting stories for a reel."
Any other answer. "Thanks. I'll keep sending the best math. Reply any time with a question."

Reply to a salary comment (written by hand or from a saved reply).
"A $[salary] salary is about [lots] lots at $450 rent and a third in expenses, before loan payments. The pack has the full sheet. Comment DEAL and I'll send it."

| Salary | Lots to match it | Math |
|---|---|---|
| $60,000 | 16.7 | 60,000 / 3,600 |
| $75,000 | 20.8 | 75,000 / 3,600 |
| $90,000 | 25 | 90,000 / 3,600 |
| $120,000 | 33.3 | 120,000 / 3,600 |

3,600 is $450 x 12 less a third for expenses. It is income before loan payments, so call it a target and not a plan.

### The free pack, two pages
1. **First step checklist, ten items**
   1. Write the monthly income you want to replace.
   2. Learn five terms, NOI, cap rate, debt service, DSCR and lot rent.
   3. Pick one property type and one market.
   4. Read ten listings and write down the income, the price and the cap rate of each.
   5. Learn what a lender checks, the property's income, your cash and credit, your experience.
   6. Meet a broker, a lender and an attorney. One call each.
   7. Ask the three questions before reading any offering.
   8. Decide how you would fund a deal, your cash, a partner or a seller note.
   9. Underwrite one deal on paper from price to cash flow.
   10. Make one call or one offer this month.
2. **Lot math sheet** a one page table with the formula and the four salaries above, plus a blank row to try your own.

Both carry the line "Illustrative numbers, educational only, not advice."

## 6. The faceless edit

- Lee fast style. The whole hook is on screen at frame 1 and spoken in the first second.
- 25 to 40 seconds. The four new scripts are about 90 to 100 words each.
- A visual change every 1.5 to 2 seconds. Counting numbers, rows that tick, a bar that fills, a card that is crossed out.
- Spoken words highlighted on screen.
- A real deal screenshot, blurred where needed, whenever Lee has one. This is the only thing no other account can copy.
- Sound. Music well under the voice, a small tick on every number and every cut.
- Cover. A big number or a short claim on a plain background, chosen on purpose and not the first frame.
- The final line echoes the first so a replay feels seamless.

## 7. Distribution without recording

- **First hour** schedule the reel, then send it to 10 to 20 real people with one question ("which number surprised you"). Build that list once, from friends, colleagues and anyone in the 484 he follows who is real.
- **Stories** each reel goes to Stories with a question sticker.
- **Comments on bigger accounts** five a day, useful and specific, on investor and operator reels in the first hour of the post. This can be done in ten minutes with a list of 20 target accounts.
- **Collabs** invite two or three small investor accounts to co author a reel.
- **Cross post the clean file** to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels. LinkedIn takes the same math as a short native video and is where lenders and buyers are.
- **No engagement pods, no bots, no bought followers.**

## 8. How we know it is working

Check after 48 hours. Write each number in a sheet.

| Number | Why |
|---|---|
| Plays, reach, share from non followers | Is it leaving his circle |
| Skip rate or 3 second hold | Did the hook work |
| Average watch time against length | Did the middle hold |
| Sends | The strongest signal for new people |
| Saves | Finance reels are saved a lot, and a save means the math was useful |
| Keyword comments and replies sent | Is the offer working |
| Profile visits and follows | Is the page converting |

Goals, as aims and not predictions.

| Stage | Aim |
|---|---|
| First 7 reels | Every reel above 100 views, the median at 300 or more, at least one reel with a send |
| Next 7 reels | One reel above 1,000 views, five or more keyword comments, first stranger follows |
| By week 8 | Most views from non followers, a repeatable format that is sent |

After seven reels keep the two series with the most sends and saves, retire the weakest, and make the next seven from the winners.
