## 2. The line, and why it sits there

Tactics I would use are in section 3. These I would not, and none of it is about being squeamish.

| Tactic | Why it is out |
|---|---|
| Fake or inflated deals, fake testimonials, "I made $X" with no proof | In real estate the product is trust. One screenshot from a skeptic ends the account. If he ever raises money, misleading claims are also a securities and advertising problem. |
| "No money down" promises that skip the catch | The classic guru red flag. The audience he wants (9 to 5ers who will actually buy) has seen it and distrusts it. |
| Planting a wrong number so people comment to correct it | It works for comments and it burns credibility the first time someone notices. Ask "what would you change" instead. |
| Bought followers, bought views, comment pods, sock puppet accounts | Against platform rules, caught by the systems that decide reach, and it gives nothing a real audience gives. |
| Copying another creator's reel word for word | Reach for a week, a takedown and a reputation as a copycat after. Use their format, never their script. |
| General solicitation of investors in public posts | If he raises money under a private offering, rules limit advertising it publicly. Teach in public, take the investor conversation private, and check this with his securities lawyer before the page grows. This is a flag, not legal advice. |

## 3. The aggressive playbook, rated

Risk is how much it can hurt him. Effort is how much work it adds.

### A. Hooks and framing

1. **Attack a popular belief with a number.** "The 1 percent rule is the minimum, not the goal." "Your 401k is not the safe choice." Pick a belief the audience repeats, then show the math. Risk low, effort low. This is the single biggest lever, and it is the one his two best reels already used.
2. **Name the argument, not the person.** "A famous finance host says never borrow. Here is what that costs you." Naming a well known claim borrows its audience and its search traffic. Criticise the idea and keep it factual, never the person. Risk medium, effort low.
3. **Loss framing.** "Every month you rent is $2,000 you will not see again." People move faster to avoid a loss than to gain a win. It is a bit manipulative and it is legal and true. Risk low, effort low.
4. **Open loops and cliffhangers.** Start with the result, delay the method. "This deal looked terrible. I bought it anyway. Here is the number that changed my mind." Only works if the payoff is real and arrives inside the reel. Risk low, effort medium.
5. **Gatekeeper teasers.** "Most brokers will not tell you this one number." Close to clickbait, so the payoff has to be genuinely useful. Risk medium, effort low.
6. **Pattern interrupts in the first second.** A number on screen at frame 1, a crossed out wrong answer, a big minus sign. The engine already does this.

### B. Engagement machines (the part that turns 100 views into 1,000)

7. **"Roast my deal".** He invites people to send a deal (price, rent, expenses) in a comment or DM, and he scores it in a reel, anonymised and with permission. Each reel creates ten new reasons for the next audience to submit. This is the strongest engine in the plan, and it uses what he already knows.
8. **Reply to comments with a video.** Every good question becomes a reel. It makes comments valuable to the asker and puts a visible question on screen.
9. **"Guess the price".** A real anonymised deal, three numbers on screen, "what did the seller want and what did I pay". Polls like this produce comments on their own.
10. **A keyword offer.** "Comment DEAL" with a DM auto reply that sends the checklist and the Zoom link. Needs the auto reply running before the next reel (Meta's rules allow one private reply per comment inside seven days). Evidence from the @flashacq reels is that at under 150 views a comment ask gets nothing, so use this only after a reel passes about 500 views, and ask for a send before that.
11. **"Send this to someone who...".** Sends are the strongest signal for reaching new people. The new share scene in the engine is for this.

### C. Borrowed reach

12. **Comment sniping.** Be the first useful, number-heavy comment (not a compliment) under big CRE and personal finance posts within ten minutes of posting, ten a day. People visit the profile of a comment that teaches. Risk low, effort 20 minutes a day.
13. **React to a viral reel with the real math.** Use Instagram's own Remix, credit the original, and say what is missing. Risk medium (do not mock people), effort medium.
14. **Collab posts.** Offer small and mid size CRE and house hacking creators a free deal check in exchange for a collab post. It shares the reel to both audiences. Offer value first.
15. **Be the guest.** Podcasts and Lives of creators his size and one level up. He has 16 years of stories, which is what hosts need.

### D. Content he already has (no recording time needed)

16. **The Wednesday talk is a clip factory.** Record every session (tell attendees at the start, get consent for anything shown). Cut 5 to 8 clips a week from questions and answers, add captions and a number on screen, post them as reels, Shorts and LinkedIn videos. This is the one answer to "he has no time to record", because the recording already happens.
17. **Fix the slot.** 3 PM Eastern on a Wednesday is when his target audience (9 to 5ers) is at work. Add an evening session or publish the replay within an hour, and say so in every reel.
18. **A running series.** "Week 3 of buying my next property", with real numbers and honest setbacks. Series are what turn viewers into followers, and no one else in the niche can copy a real one. Only do this with real facts.

### E. Distribution

19. **Same clean master everywhere** (no watermark) to Instagram, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook Reels and LinkedIn. Each reel is five lottery tickets.
20. **LinkedIn is where the buyers and operators are.** Carousels of the same deal math, 6 to 8 slides, one number per slide, posted from his own name. He has the credentials there that Instagram does not show.
21. **Pin and re-use.** Re-post the best reel every six weeks with a new first second. Reels that worked once usually work again.

## 4. Fix the funnel first (one afternoon)

The traffic is pointless until these exist.
1. **Instagram name field** "Lee | Real Estate Investing" and a bio, for example "16 years in real estate. Real deal math in 30 seconds. Free weekly talk, Wednesdays." and the site as the link.
2. **The site needs an email capture.** Right now the only action is "join the free discussion". Add a one page checklist (a deal analysis sheet) for an email, and send the Zoom reminder from that list. Owned contacts are worth more than followers.
3. **Add the replay and a clip reel to the site**, with a line on who it is for ("for people with a 9 to 5 who want to own commercial real estate").
4. **One keyword**, DEAL, in the bio, the caption, the last two lines of every script and the auto reply.

## 5. The 30 day operating loop

Five reels a week, one per day Monday to Friday, never two in a day.
- Mon number reel from the engine (one calculation you can follow out loud).
- Tue reply to a comment with a video.
- Wed no reel. The talk runs, and recording starts.
- Thu clip from the talk, with a number on screen.
- Fri roast a deal, or guess the price.
- Daily 20 minutes of comment sniping, and every comment on his own reels answered inside an hour.
- Weekly LinkedIn carousel from Monday's number reel.

**Test rule.** Keep a sheet of every reel with its views, 3 second hold, average watch time and sends. After two weeks drop the two weakest hook shapes and repeat the best one. The evidence so far says the best shape is a number plus a concrete case.

**What to measure** (Insights). Average watch time against reel length, sends, and the share of views from non-followers. Follower count is a lagging number.

## 6. Honest expectations

Nothing here promises a viral reel. With 277 followers every reel is a test on strangers, and most tests fail. What the plan does is raise the odds on each one (a stronger hook shape, a reason to send), put a funnel under the traffic that arrives (a bio, a site with email capture) and add a content source (the weekly talk) that does not depend on him finding time to record. A realistic target for the first 30 days is one reel past 1,000 views and 300 to 500 new followers. Past that, the compounding comes from the series and the collabs.
