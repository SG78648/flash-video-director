# Lee reels 23 and 24, broader topics

The last batch (the $200 below market rent, the $550 underwriting) is true and specific, and it needs a viewer who already knows what a rent roll is. A stranger scrolling does not. These two use topics anyone has lived. Starting, procrastinating, feeling stuck, working yourself flat. They are still built from Lee's own public posts, so every fact is his.

## Honest note on the evidence

- **Jeff Ervick.** I could not read his LinkedIn posts (logged out, the profile and feed are blocked), so I cannot say which of his posts went viral. What is public is his positioning and themes. Real estate should complement a W-2 job and not replace it ("I didn't get into real estate to get out of my W2"), use your steady income to fund investing, time block your days for work, family and balance, communicate more than feels necessary, treat investors as co-owners, and mentor others because "someone did it for me". His headline also names one kind of viewer. The pattern to steal is the theme level (a universal tension, a named viewer, a calm contrarian turn), not any wording. If you send me links to his top posts, I will replace this with a real read.
- **Lee's own data.** His broad, human posts did not do better than his deal and story posts on LinkedIn. Burnout reached 260 impressions, "the fastest way to stay broke" 191, "some people don't want to build wealth" 159, against 695 to 1,900 for the $527 story and the boring investor post. LinkedIn readers are peers and brokers, though, and reels go to strangers, where a universal hook has a wider door. So this is a bet on broader hooks for reels, not something his LinkedIn numbers prove. The reels will tell us in a week.

**Where each fact comes from (Lee to confirm)**
- 23 follows his post on discouragement. Repeated disappointment erodes belief that effort matters, discouraged people procrastinate, overthink, doubt themselves and avoid opportunities, one real win can show change is possible, and "confidence is built from proof, not positive thinking". The line "so the fix isn't a pep talk, it's a small win you can actually get this week" is my extension of that idea, so he should say if he agrees. It also states a view and not a medical claim. Keep the caption as opinion.
- 24 follows his posts on beginners staying stuck and on wealth building without action. Review 20 deals, talk to 5 people (a broker, an investor, a lender), make 1 offer that teaches more than 50 videos. "Look at 20 deals" can become "20 examples" if the reel runs outside real estate. The line about books, podcasts and experts comes from his "some people don't want to build wealth" post, which also says to start small and while still employed.
