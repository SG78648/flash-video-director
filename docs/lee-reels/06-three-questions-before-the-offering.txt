Before I read a park's offering, I ask three questions.
First, who owns the homes.
In many parks the tenants own the homes and you own the land.
Second, who pays for the water and sewer.
A private system can be very expensive to fix.
Third, how much of the income is real.
Ask for the rent actually collected over the last 12 months, not the rent on paper.
Plenty of bad deals fail on one of these three.
And you can check all of them before you spend a dollar.
So don't start with the pretty summary.
Start with the questions.
