# FlashAcq, the next two reels

Scripts 06 and 07 in `docs/flashacq-scripts/`, written after reading the account on 2026-10-11 (logged out, so only what Instagram shows to a visitor).
Both paste straight into **New project > Script**. No script can promise a viral reel. These two are built to give a cold audience the best chance, and the plan below makes sure you can tell what worked.

## What the account shows today

- 23 followers, 31 following, 6 reels.
- Views per reel, newest to oldest as the grid lists them 82, 37, 24, 54, 92, 127. The oldest reel (September 15) has the most.
- Likes visible on the reel pages are 1 to 3, with no comments on any of them. All six use original audio.
- Bio reads "Turning Properties into Possibilities. We invest in SFHs and MFHs. Partner with us!" with a link page.
- The reel pages showed no caption text to a logged out visitor, and Insights (watch time, shares, where views came from) cannot be seen from outside. So this is a read of the surface, not of the numbers behind it.

## What it points to

1. **With 23 followers, almost every viewer is a stranger.** Instagram shows a reel to a small test group first and widens it only if they watch and react. 24 to 127 views means the test group did not react enough to widen it.
2. **The first five reels explain investor maths** (cap rate, scale premium, value add). That teaches people who already invest. A stranger scrolling has no reason to stop.
3. **They run about 55 to 70 seconds and end on "follow me".** Long reels lose viewers before the payoff, and "follow me" gives no reason to act. There is nothing to comment on, which matches the empty comment sections.
4. **The bio speaks to partners, not to viewers.** Nobody who lands there knows what they get from following.

## Numbers to check before posting

06
- $400,000 x 3.5% = $14,000. FHA allows 3.5 percent down with a credit score of about 580 or more, on a 1 to 4 unit home you live in. Check the current terms and your county loan limit.
- The $3,200 a month is an illustration. About $2,450 loan payment (6.5 percent, 30 years, roughly $386,000 borrowed), about $500 taxes and about $250 insurance and mortgage insurance. Change any of them and the answer moves.
- The other side renting for $2,000 is a market assumption. Say it in the caption.
- Some lenders count only part of the rent when you apply. Say "terms vary" in the caption.

07
- $4,000 a month is 4 x $1,000, and $3,000 is 75 percent of it. Real fourplexes carry bigger expenses than a house, so say "illustrative, before expenses".
- $2,500 for one house and $1,000 a unit for a fourplex are examples for a lower cost market. Swap them for the market you buy in.

## How to post these two

1. Post 06 first and 07 the next day. 06 has the wider audience. 07 keeps the people who stayed.
2. Post at the time your followers are most active (Insights, Total followers, Most active times). With 23 followers the first hour is still the test, so be there.
3. Share each reel to your Story and send it to 10 to 20 real people with one question ("would you buy the house or the fourplex?"). A send counts for more than a like.
4. Reply to every comment inside the first hour, each with a question back.
5. Fix the bio today, for example "Helping renters become owners. Real deal math in 40 seconds. Comment HACK on any reel." Keep the link.
6. Set up the HACK auto reply before 06 goes up. If it is not ready, reply to each comment yourself.

## What to look at after 48 hours

Insights on each reel. These tell you whether the script or the packaging was the problem.
- **Average watch time against reel length** below about 50 percent means the middle is too slow. Shorten it.
- **Shares and saves** matter more than likes. High shares with few views means the hook is good and the reel just needs more tests.
- **Views from non-followers** is the number that should climb. If 06 or 07 gets 500 or more views while the older reels got under 130, the new style is working and the next reels should copy it.
