The scariest part of house hacking is the empty unit, so let's put a price on it.
[hook icon warning]
Say the duplex costs about $3,295 a month and the other side rents for $1,800, so a normal month costs you about $1,495.
[$3,295 - $1,800 = $1,495 a month]
Now say the tenant leaves and the unit sits empty for two months, which costs you an extra $3,600 over the year.
[$1,495 x 12 + $3,600 = $21,540]
Even then the year comes to about $21,500, which is still less than the $24,000 you'd pay renting at $2,000 a month.
[$2,000 x 12 = $24,000]
Just keep a few months of that rent saved before you buy.
