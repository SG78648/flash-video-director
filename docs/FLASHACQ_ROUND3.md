## What it points to

1. **The scenario-and-numbers hook did about three times better than the broad hook** (120 against 41 in the same window). With numbers this small it is a hint, not proof, but it is the only signal on the page, so the next two keep that shape. A concrete case, two outcomes, a number.
2. **Nobody commented, even with a keyword and a poll.** At 40 to 120 views, a comment ask cannot work. The final ask should be one that costs a viewer nothing and counts for more, which is sending the reel to someone. Sends are one of the strongest signals Instagram uses to widen a reel. Both new scripts end with a send ask, and the closing card says it too.
3. **Two reels an hour apart share the same small test audience.** The second one competes with the first. Post one reel a day.
4. **Followers did not move at all.** Strangers who watch to the end have no reason to press follow, because the profile does not say what they get. The bio is the cheapest fix left.

## Numbers to check before posting

08
- 1 percent of $200,000 is $2,000. Half of $2,000 is $1,000. A loan of $160,000 (20 percent down) at 6.5 percent over 30 years is $1,011 a month, so $1,000 minus $1,011 is minus $11.
- At $2,400, half is $1,200, minus $1,011 leaves $189.
- The 50 percent for taxes, insurance and repairs is a common rule of thumb. Real costs vary a lot by place and by building. Say so in the caption.

09
- 16 units at $250 is $4,000. The $250 a unit after the loan, taxes, insurance, repairs and empty months is an illustration, not a typical result. Cash flow per unit varies widely.
- 25 percent down is a common minimum for an investment loan, and lenders differ. Check the current terms.
- A fourplex at $400,000 is an example for a lower cost market.

  ```
  Send this to someone who's about to buy a rental.
  A $200,000 house that rents for $2,000 passes the 1 percent rule and still comes up $11 short every month. At $2,400 it keeps $189.
  ```

## How to post

1. Post 08 tomorrow and 09 the day after. Not on the same day.
2. Change the bio before 08 goes up, for example "Real deal math in 30 seconds. SFH and multifamily. Send a reel to someone who needs it." and keep the link.
3. Share each reel to your Story and send it yourself to 10 to 20 people with a question ("would you buy this?").
4. Answer every comment inside the first hour, with a question back.
5. Check Insights after 48 hours. Average watch time against length and shares tell you more than likes. If 08 or 09 passes about 300 views, repeat that hook shape for the next reels.
