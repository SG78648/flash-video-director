## Numbers to check before posting

10
- $150,000 + $30,000 = $180,000. $240,000 x 75 percent = $180,000. The result ignores closing costs, holding costs while the house is empty, and refinance fees, so it is a best case. Say so in the caption.
- Many lenders refinance at 70 to 75 percent of value on an investment property, and some want the house held for a few months first (a seasoning period). Check current terms.
- The first $180,000 has to come from somewhere (cash or a short term loan with its own cost).

11
- $6,000 + $5,000 + $2,000 = $13,000. $13,000 / $400 = 32.5 months. The $2,000 rent and $400 profit are illustrations.
- Eviction time, legal costs and repair bills differ a lot by state and by tenant. Use a range in the caption.
- Screening rules (fair housing law, what you may ask) differ by place. The $50 is a typical fee for a credit and background check.

## How to post

1. One reel a day, not two. Post 11 first (the scene hook is closest to what worked) and 10 the day after.
2. Fix the bio before 11 goes up, for example "Real deal math in 30 seconds. SFH and multifamily. Send a reel to someone who needs it." and keep the link.
3. Share each reel to your Story and send it to 10 to 20 people with a question. Answer every comment inside the first hour.
4. If a reel passes about 300 views, repeat its hook shape in the next one. If neither does, the next test should be the same hooks with faces or real photos of a house, not new maths.
